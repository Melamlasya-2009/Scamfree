# 🛡️ ScamFree

### Check Before You Trust.

ScamFree is a web-based digital safety platform designed to help users identify suspicious online activities, report potential scams, track reports, and improve awareness about digital fraud.

## 💡 About the Project

ScamFree was conceptualized as a college ideathon project to address a common real-world problem: people often encounter suspicious payment requests, online sellers, social-media accounts, links, and other digital interactions without knowing whether they can be trusted.

The platform brings useful safety features together in one simple interface.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔍 **Scam Check** – Check suspicious digital information and view a risk indication.
* 🚨 **Report Scam** – Report suspicious accounts, transactions, websites, or online activities.
* 📊 **Track Reports** – Follow the status of submitted reports.
* ❓ **Q&A** – Find answers to common scam-related questions.
* 🛡️ **Scam Awareness** – Learn about common online scam patterns and safety practices.
* 📱 **User-Friendly Interface** – Designed to make digital safety simple and accessible.

## 🎯 Problem

Online scams are becoming increasingly common across digital payments, social media, shopping platforms, websites, and messaging services. Many users struggle to identify suspicious activity before becoming a victim.

## 💡 Our Approach

ScamFree aims to provide users with a simple safety layer where they can check suspicious information, report potential scams, and learn how to stay safer online.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

ScamFree is an educational and project-based platform. A risk indication should not be treated as a guaranteed determination that something is genuine or fraudulent. Users should verify important information through trusted sources before making payments or sharing sensitive information.
