      How it works

      Six steps between you and a safer payment.

      01

      Scan or enter

      Paste a UPI ID, scan a QR code, or drop in a seller name, website or Instagram handle.

      02

      ScamFree checks

      We cross-reference the identity against community reports, activity signals and payment history.

      03

      Risk result

      Get an instant read: low risk, caution, or high risk — with the signals behind the score.

      04

      You decide

      ScamFree never blocks a payment. You always make the final call, with more information than before.

      05

      Order tracking

      If the seller provides details, track delivery and follow-up in one place.

      06

      Report & help

      If something goes wrong, report it — your report helps flag risk for the next buyer.
